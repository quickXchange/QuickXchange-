---
name: Public trust claims
description: Accuracy boundary for customer-facing financial, legal, affiliate, and contact content.
---

Customer-facing pages must use only business details and operational claims that are verified in the product or supplied by the business. Do not invent contact details, social destinations, review profiles, affiliate percentages, availability promises, security certifications, audit practices, licenses, or compliance tooling.

**Why:** Plausible placeholder copy can look authoritative on a financial website and mislead customers. A visibly incomplete destination is safer than a polished but false claim.

**How to apply:** Reuse live Admin-managed content and actual product contracts where available. Otherwise use careful general wording, keep unsupported external links inactive, and request official details as a separate follow-up.
