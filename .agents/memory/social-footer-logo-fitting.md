---
name: Social footer logo fitting
description: Circular cover fitting for uploaded social logos, distinct from whole-mark catalog containment.
---

Uploaded social footer logos fill the entire uniformly sized circular container in both themes, Live Preview and the public footer. Compensate for transparent canvas padding and center the visible artwork. Cropping is permitted to fill the circle; stretching is not.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested full-circle social artwork rather than a small centered icon surrounded by empty space, while prohibiting footer redesign, link/settings changes, or added white/black square backgrounds.

**How to apply:** Keep this cover-fitting policy scoped to social footer uploads. Do not apply it to Trustpilot wordmarks, partner logos, or payment-method/catalog logos that require whole-mark containment. Preserve uploaded files and theme-specific image selection; adjust rendering only.
