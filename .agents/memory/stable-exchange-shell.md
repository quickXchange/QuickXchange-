---
name: Stable exchange shell
description: Every Swap and Convert step shares the measured Swap Step 1 outer footprint; long form fields alone may scroll.
---

On Landing and Customer Dashboard, every Swap and Convert step keeps the same fixed outer size as the current Swap Step 1 at each responsive breakpoint. Normal content should fit through spacing and internal layout, not forced scrolling. When admin-configured payment fields or provider details exceed that space, the user explicitly prefers keeping the outer size fixed and scrolling only the overflowing field area; the route summary, terms, and action controls must remain visible. Optional Add-ons controls belong on Summary, not Step 1; the fee itemization card is intentionally absent from Summary even though signed quote fees and calculations remain authoritative. Open selector result lists retain their own scrolling.

**Why:** An earlier natural-height approach protected Step 1 and Summary from clipping, but caused visible outer-size jumps during navigation. The owner now prioritizes a fixed Swap Step 1 footprint across all steps, explicitly accepting field-list-only scrolling for unusually long forms rather than changing the outer dimensions. The owner separately requested removing fee itemization from Step 3's UI only, not changing pricing or order behavior. Layered shared input/button dimensions can silently defeat later compact overrides.

**How to apply:** Keep viewport, active layer, and card dimensions aligned through Swap/Convert switches and Next/Back, using the rendered Swap Step 1 footprint at each breakpoint as reference. Fit normal quote and Summary content without internal scrolling, with fallback overflow only where necessary; long Step 2 field lists can scroll independently while terms and submit stay outside. Keep width, border, glow, and control sizes consistent with Convert. Keep Add-ons selectable from Summary without restoring the fee breakdown or changing calculations or backend capabilities. Terminal availability states are a separate mobile exception: short notices can release the fixed shell. Verify every field and persistent support control remains keyboard reachable and hit-testable. Update final owning CSS rules when earlier layered or important declarations conflict.
