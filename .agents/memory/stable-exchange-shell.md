---
name: Stable exchange shell
description: Swap Step 1 and Summary expand naturally without internal scrolling; fulfillment and Convert keep bounded shell behavior.
---

On Landing and Customer Dashboard, active Swap Step 1 and Step 3 Summary expand vertically with their content at every breakpoint. Neither should scroll internally; the webpage may scroll. Step 2 fulfillment and Convert retain bounded shell behavior. Fee itemization and optional Add-ons controls belong on Summary, not Step 1. When Step 2's required payment-field set cannot fit, only the field list may scroll; the route summary, terms, and submit action remain fixed and visible. Open selector result lists retain their own scrolling.

**Why:** Fixed Step 1 clipped its quote content, and fixed Step 3 squeezed Summary sections and actions into an overlapping internal scrollport. The owner requires both steps to grow naturally, with the page—not the widget—handling excess height. The bounded shell still protects variable-length fulfillment fields and Convert. Layered shared input/button dimensions can silently defeat later compact overrides.

**How to apply:** Release viewport, active layer, card, and panel height together for Step 1 and Summary rather than merely hiding a scrollbar. Keep width, border, glow, and control sizes consistent with Convert. Keep Add-ons selectable from Summary without changing fee calculations or backend capabilities. For long Step 2 payment-field sets, scroll only the field list and keep terms and submit outside it. Terminal availability states are a separate mobile exception: release viewport, layer, and card height together so short notices do not leave empty space. Verify every field and persistent support control remains keyboard reachable and hit-testable. Update final owning CSS rules when earlier layered or important declarations conflict.
